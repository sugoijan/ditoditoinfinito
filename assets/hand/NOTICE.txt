Dancing hand runtime asset notices

The hand mesh in hand.glb derives from Dan Ulrich's Hand - Realistic in
Blender Human Base Meshes v1.4.1 (CC0 1.0,
https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/). MZiemys' skeleton (CC0 1.0,
https://blendswap.com/blend/17281) was a reference for its bones. The
modeling, rigging, skin weights and corrective morphs, the calibration in
model.json and the code are by すごいジャン (MIT).

The studies that made model.json planned the hand's motion on Beatucada's
charts: chart by Timo Kitsune, from OutFox Serenity, CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The development studies also compared the hand against material that is in
none of these files: WAVEFILE's MikuMikuDance motion (by hino), a reference
Hatsune Miku model, and the salsa CMU60_01 from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion
Capture Database (mocap.cs.cmu.edu). The hand has no Miku geometry, and
these files hold no recorded dance or motion capture: the GLB's one
animation track is a static carrier of two identical zero keys. The hand
and its calibration are original work that does not meaningfully use
WAVEFILE's motion; the Miku model and the CMU salsa served only the
studies' comparisons and previews (the maintainer's assessment, 2026-11-06).
