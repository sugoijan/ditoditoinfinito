# 日本語: terms and tone

For whoever translates or reviews the Japanese messages, people and agents
alike. Changes to this list are the maintainer's.

## Tone

- Sentences (descriptions, notices, confirmations) in です・ます, plain
  and short. Avoid piling up 〜してください; prefer a statement of what
  happens (「曲が削除されます。」).
- Buttons and menu items as short nouns or verb stems: 決定, 戻る, 保存,
  削除, 再生.
- Japanese punctuation in Japanese sentences: 。、「」（）. Half-width
  digits and Latin letters.
- A Latin word or name inside Japanese keeps no extra spaces around it
  unless English text follows.

## Never translated

- The game's name: Dito Dito Infinito (`-game-name`).
- Judgments and grades: they come from the chosen Judge (the ruleset), not
  from the language, and read the same in every language. So do the
  game's readouts: score, percentage, combo, offsets in ms. The one
  exception is DDI's own judgment words, below, which the player may see
  in their language (Options → Rules → Judgment words, on by default);
  its grades (Absolute Dito, Absolute Bot, SSS … D, F) and lamps stay
  Latin.
- The results' marks beside the lamps: REPLAY, AUTOPLAY, ASSIST (with
  the grades, FAILED and QUIT).
- Charts' difficulty names, as StepMania's simfiles name them: Beginner,
  Easy, Medium, Hard, Challenge, Edit.
- Key names as keycaps print them: ENTER, ESC, TAB, SPACE, the arrows.
- BPM; file formats and other games' names (StepMania, .sm, .ssc,
  Dancing☆Onigiri).

## DDI's judgment words (round 4, an unverified draft)

`judgments.ftl`. Katakana, as the game's judgments read in Japanese
games; Dancing☆Onigiri's words (イイ, シャキン, マターリ) are the
reference for tone where a transliteration reads badly. The hidden
hint carries over: in English every late word has a G; here **every
late word has a voicing mark (゛) and no early word has one**. W0, Miss
and Boo are neither early nor late (アブソリュート and ブー have a ゛ of
their own; nothing hints by them).

| Tier | English early | 日本語 early | English late | 日本語 late |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W0 | Absolute | アブソリュート | (the same) | (the same) |
| W1 | Awesome | オーサム | Amazing | アメージング |
| W2 | Fantastic | ファンタスティック | Glorious | グロリアス |
| W3 | Nice | ナイス | Great | グレート |
| W4 | OK | オーケー | Good | グッド |
| W5 | Too early | ハヤトチリ | Too late | マニアワズ |
| Miss | Miss | ミス | | |
| Boo (and the results' count, Boos) | Boo | ブー | | |
| A hold held to its end (2026-11-13, unverified draft) | Held | キープ | | |
| A hold dropped (2026-11-13, unverified draft) | Off | オフ | | |

W5 is not transliterated: トゥーアーリー reads badly, and the plain
ハヤスギ / オソスギ would put a ゛ in the early word. ハヤトチリ (早とちり,
jumping the gun) and マニアワズ (間に合わず, did not make it) say it in
the Dancing☆Onigiri spirit and keep the hint. Unsure: whether those two
read naturally as judgments to a native player; オーサム (rare in
Japanese) and アブソリュート (long) might want other words. The hold
outcomes (since 2026-11-13): キープ (kept down to the end) and オフ (let
off), plain game katakana, neither early nor late; Dancing☆Onigiri's own
キター / イクナイ were the tone's reference but read as net slang. English
may rename Off to "Dropped" (one message, `judgments-dropped`): ドロップ
would follow.

## Review decisions (2026-11-08, approved by the maintainer)

- Importing adds content to the library: インポート. Loading content already
  present is 読み込み. An action called "Add songs" remains 曲を追加.
- Calibration's full name remains キャリブレーション; the shared short action
  "Calibrate" is 調整 so it fits the footer's buttons.
- Receptors remain 判定ライン: the shared term for the stationary targets in
  every layout, without adopting another game's ステップゾーン terminology.
- Results remain リザルト for a play's screen; generic outcomes can be 結果.
  Speed remains スピード for scrolling; playback rate is 再生速度. Do not
  replace either with ハイスピード, which suggests only speeding up.
- Hiding library entries remains 隠す. macOS app-menu items follow the
  platform's 非表示 (including ほかを非表示); this is an intentional exception.
- Preserve Pump and Techno in style names, as the English source requests.
  Key-hint caps may be separated from their action by a space; this is
  layout, not a space around a Latin word within a Japanese sentence.
- A device whose offset a test measured is 測定済み (Options' device rows,
  calibration's list), as against 既定値; one not measured is 未測定. The
  action stays 調整 and the start prompt's hint タイミング未調整, which
  sits beside that button.

## The player themselves: 自分, and dropping あなた (2026-11-14)

- A list's own row ("You": the players panel, the Players pop-up, the
  stage's prompt, the Standings) and the badge after the player's own
  signed-in name are 自分, as Japanese game UIs label one's own row;
  あなた addresses someone and reads as a stranger's label there. The
  same for a label of one's own thing (自分の録音).
- Sentences that address the player drop the pronoun where Japanese
  naturally would (このルームのホストです, 選曲を担当しています, 対戦は退出後も
  続きます) rather than translate "you" each time. あなた stays only where
  the sentence needs it to tell the player apart from someone else named
  in it (room-make-host-body: "{ $name }がルームを管理し、あなたは共同ホスト
  になります").
