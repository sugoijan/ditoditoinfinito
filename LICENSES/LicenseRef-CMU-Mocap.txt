CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database usage terms
Source: https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
Retrieved: 2026-10-30

The database permits all uses, including research and inclusion in
commercially sold products. Direct resale of the motion data is prohibited,
including resale in a converted format. This is not a CC0 dedication.

The site requests acknowledgement of mocap.cs.cmu.edu and NSF EIA-0196217,
which funded creation of the database, when publishing results. It also
requests a citation of published papers be sent to the database maintainers.

An archived copy of the source page accompanies Study088. This identifier
records those source-specific permissions and restrictions; it does not
replace or broaden them.
